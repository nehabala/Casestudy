The Brief

We need more warm, qualified leads for targeted sales calls.

Interview Kickstart offers high-ticket courses, so customer acquisition cost was steep. They needed a way to attract and pre-qualify serious candidates before any sales conversation began.

The Output

What shipped

What this tool does

AI-powered resume analysis — Score Analysis, Gap Analysis, Objective Analysis, and Formatting & ATS Compatibility delivered instantly.

Role-targeted personalisation — roles suggested from the resume itself, or the user pastes a JD for a more specific report.

Loading state as lead capture — while the API processes (~20 sec), users play a Chrome dino homage and fill in their contact details.

10-day action plan — unlocks after analysis, day-by-day prep guide with tips from named FAANG instructors.

Webinar CTA — slot booking appears contextually and as an exit-intent popup to re-capture disengaged users.

Fully adaptive — not just responsive. Mobile layout reorganises hierarchy for thumb reach, not just stacked columns.

Process

How we got here

01

Competitor Landscape

I reviewed the top resume analysis tools at that time — Resume Worded, Kickresume, Enhancv, Wellfound. All followed the same pattern:

Upload Resume
→
Share Contact Details
→
Wait for Analysis (Minutes / days)
→
View / Receive Generic Report
"Analyses seemed generic, with long wait times and confusing reports."
02

Understanding the User

I looked at user reviews, discussion threads on Reddit, and our alumni Discord. I also met with 5 senior tech professionals from both India & US in my network who fit the target group.

User quotes
"

I don't really use these tools, I prefer insights from real people.

→ Make it feel human, not AI-generated
"

I want to know exactly what part of my resume triggers the ATS to reject it.

→ ATS-specific analysis section
"

I don't really get actionable feedback most times.

→ Prescriptive, not just descriptive
Design implications
Targeted insights on what an ATS expects
Advice inspired by real FAANG experiences
Compare resume against the target role, not a generic benchmark
Go beyond analysis — show how to translate it into a good interview
Most importantly — do not make it feel like generic advice generated by AI.
03

Defining the User Flow

Mapped the end-to-end flow from landing to webinar sign-up, designing each stage around what the user needs to feel confident enough to proceed.

04

Creating an Easy-to-Digest Analysis

Although the outline of the content was given to us by the curriculum team, I collaborated with them to group the topics into logical categories, name them, and set sentence limits to pass onto the core prompt.

Score Analysis
A brief overview to sum up the whole analysis in quantifiable scores
Gap Analysis
Highlight what we're comparing the resume to, and exactly where it lags
Objective Analysis
How to reframe resume content in a more impactful way
Formatting & ATS Compatibility
Addressing areas at risk of ATS rejection and any left-out errors
Responses were to be modelled on information & interview feedback from FAANG mock interviewers
05

Designing the UI

I stuck to IK's blue theme and colour palette, keeping it minimal, but trying to break the seriousness with mascots and little quirks — most notably the dino character, which carries the brand personality across the entire tool.

Key Design Decision

Overcoming the hurdle of latency — literally.

Tech team mentioned cases where it took ~20 seconds for the API response to reach us — exactly when users would be most likely to leave. I suggested a mini game — an homage to Chrome's dino, instantly recognisable to anyone in tech. It broke the ice while we captured missing contact details and showed resume tips.

Performance & Impact

Data from Clarity analytics

I went through the Clarity analytics dashboard and tracked 90 days of data from Q2 2026. Here are some key indicators of performance.

Landing page
⭐
Key action is clear Good

Upload & Proceed are the highest-clicked elements — 160, 153, 136 clicks respectively. Retain this visual hierarchy.

⭐
91.3% hit 50% scroll depth Good

On average across both pages — strong signal of user engagement with the content.

☹
4.42% reach Proceed Investigate

Most don't advance far enough to become leads. Possible role choice overload at the targeting step.

☹
9.35% dead-click rate Resolved

Later discovered as elements firing with latency, and a few cases of tags being clicked on. Edited in later versions after reviewing 30+ session recordings.

Analysis page
☹
Only 17.4% reach bottom Action needed

91.3% hit 50% depth but only 17.4% reach the bottom. Important webinar CTAs should not depend on bottom-of-page exposure.

☹
Low webinar CTA interaction Context

Doesn't make sense to click when they are reading the analysis. CTA placement needs to follow the analysis journey, not sit above it.

How would I redesign it with what I know now?

Taking insights from Clarity data, and quick usability tests with people in my network.

Redesign elements that are seen to cause dead clicks
Make the page visually less monotonous
Add more details about the webinar, to increase curiosity
Remove some input methods in the role selection step
Use a more conversational method of resume collection
Use visuals and animations, to make it less wordy
